Privacy Policy

RateVault is a currency converter made by VaultApps. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your device, and what happens when you use online or external features.

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Summary

RateVault does not require an account, does not show ads, does not use analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, calendar, files or device advertising ID. The amounts you type stay on your device.

Who we are

RateVault is developed by VaultApps, which is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can contact VaultApps at support@vaultapps.org.

Data stored on your device

RateVault saves your selected currency cards and their order, your theme, whether you have seen the tutorial, and the latest verified exchange rates with their dates.

On Android this is app-local storage. If Android backup is turned on, Android's encrypted backup and device-transfer features can include it so it is restored on a new device. Those copies are managed by Android and your Google account, not by VaultApps.

In the web app it is browser local storage for the site, and the browser also caches the app's files so it can reopen offline after a successful online visit.

This data stays until it is replaced, you clear the app or site data, or you uninstall the app. Rates bundled with the app are used until newer verified rates are saved.

Typed conversion amounts

Amounts you type are used only to calculate the conversions on screen. They are processed on your device and are never sent to VaultApps or the exchange-rate provider. RateVault does not save typed amounts to storage, so they are never backed up. After you clear amounts or delete a card they are kept in memory briefly so you can undo, and they are gone when the app is closed.

If you copy an amount, your device places it on the clipboard, where other apps you use may be able to read it. RateVault reads the clipboard only when you choose to paste.

Exchange rate updates

To get exchange rates, RateVault sends an HTTPS request to Exchange Rate API at open.er-api.com. The request asks for rates against the US dollar and contains no amounts, preferences, contact details or identifiers. RateVault checks the status, base currency, date, completeness and values of each response before using it.

Like any internet service, the provider and network operators receive technical information needed to answer the request, such as your IP address and the time. Exchange Rate API's terms, including how it handles this information, are at exchangerate-api.com/terms.

Web app hosting

The web app's files are served by Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard request information such as IP address, browser, requested page and time to deliver and protect the site (see cloudflare.com/privacypolicy). RateVault adds no analytics, advertising trackers or advertising cookies. Fonts, currency symbols and flags are served from the same site rather than an external font service.

External links and contact

Email support and feature suggestions open your email app with a message to support@vaultapps.org. If you send it, VaultApps receives your email address, the message and anything you include.

Store reviews open Google Play (policies.google.com/privacy), Ko-fi opens in your browser or the Ko-fi app (more.ko-fi.com/privacy), and the rate provider's link opens its website. These services are not controlled by RateVault and handle information under their own policies. RateVault does not process payments.

How data is used

RateVault uses data only to convert currencies, keep recent rates available offline and remember your preferences. When you email VaultApps, your message is used to reply to you, on the basis of VaultApps' legitimate interest in answering your request. RateVault does not sell personal information or share it for advertising or tracking.

Data retention and deletion

Use "Clear saved preferences" in Settings to reset your currency cards, theme and tutorial; saved rates are kept. To remove everything, clear RateVault's app data or uninstall it on Android, or clear the site's data in your browser. Android backups follow your device backup settings and can be turned off in Android settings. Browsers may also remove offline data when storage is low.

Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you, keep support records or meet legal obligations. You can ask VaultApps to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the UK or EU GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information that VaultApps holds about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because RateVault keeps app data on your device, you can view and remove it there. For support emails or any other privacy request, contact support@vaultapps.org.

Permissions

On Android, RateVault requests only internet access, to download exchange rates. It does not request location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, SMS or call log access. The web app uses ordinary browser network and storage features and does not ask for location, camera or microphone access.

Security

Exchange rates are downloaded over HTTPS, and the web app is served with security headers that stop it being embedded in other sites or loading code from them. Local data uses your device's normal app or browser storage. RateVault does not store passwords, payment details, government identifiers or account credentials.

Children's privacy

RateVault is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. VaultApps does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

VaultApps may update this policy if RateVault's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above changes whenever the policy changes materially.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact VaultApps at support@vaultapps.org.
