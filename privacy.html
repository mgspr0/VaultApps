Privacy Policy

RateVault is an offline-enabled currency converter by VaultApps.

Effective date: June 20, 2026

Summary

RateVault does not require an account, does not show ads, does not use analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, calendar, files, or device advertising ID.

Data Stored on Your Device

RateVault stores a small amount of app data locally on your device so the converter can work offline and remember your preferences:

  • Cached exchange rates, the provider-published rate date, and the last fetch time.
  • Your selected currency cards and their order.
  • Your theme preference.
  • Whether you have completed or skipped the introductory walkthrough.

Local app data remains on your device until it is replaced by updated data, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall the app.

Typed Conversion Amounts

Amounts typed into the converter are processed on your device and are not sent to VaultApps or the exchange-rate service. RateVault uses them only to calculate the conversions visible in the app while you use it. They are not saved to device storage by RateVault and are cleared when you clear the fields, remove the app from memory, or restart the app.

Exchange-Rate Updates

RateVault uses an internet connection to download exchange rates from:

https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest

These requests are used only to update currency data, and RateVault verifies the response before saving it locally.

RateVault does not send your typed amounts, selected currencies, theme preference, contact details, account identifiers, or precise location to the exchange-rate service. Like most internet services, the exchange-rate provider and network operators may receive standard technical information needed to deliver the response, such as your IP address, request time, and basic device or connection information.

External Links and Contact

Some actions open services outside RateVault:

  • Email support or feature suggestions open your email app and prepare a message to support@vaultapps.org. If you send the email, VaultApps receives your email address, message content, and any information you choose to include.
  • Store ratings open the app store listing.
  • Ko-fi support opens Ko-fi in your browser or the Ko-fi app.

These external services are not controlled by RateVault. Any information you provide to your email provider, app store, Ko-fi, browser, or payment provider is handled under that service's own privacy policy. RateVault does not process payments inside the app.

How Data Is Used

RateVault uses data only to:

  • Provide currency conversions.
  • Keep recent exchange rates available offline.
  • Remember your app preferences.
  • Respond to support messages you choose to send.

RateVault does not sell personal data and does not share personal data for advertising or tracking.

Data Retention and Deletion

On-device data can be deleted by clearing RateVault's app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Cached exchange rates may also be replaced automatically when new rates are downloaded.

Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond to your request, maintain support records, or meet legal obligations. You can ask VaultApps to delete support correspondence by emailing support@vaultapps.org.

Permissions

On Android, RateVault requests internet access so it can download exchange rates and open external links. The app does not request sensitive permissions such as location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, SMS, or call log access.

Security

Exchange-rate updates are requested over HTTPS. Local data is stored using the device's normal app storage mechanisms. RateVault does not store passwords, payment card details, government identifiers, or account credentials.

Children's Privacy

RateVault is a general-audience utility app and is not directed to children. RateVault does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

VaultApps may update this policy if RateVault's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact VaultApps at support@vaultapps.org.
